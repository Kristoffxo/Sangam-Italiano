### Hours as listed on Google — NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION
Google shows, for all seven days: `12 am–12 pm` and `1 pm–12 am`.
That reads as open 23 hours with a one-hour break at noon, which is almost
certainly a data-entry error on the owner's Google profile.
The press release says `11:00 AM – 10:00 PM`.
**These two sources conflict. Get the real hours from the owner before publishing.**

## Brand story / positioning
Source: a single paid advertorial (VMPL press release) syndicated to ANI, The
Tribune, and Lokmat Times. This is the restaurant's own marketing copy, not
independent reporting — treat claims accordingly.

- Founder: Chef Kushal Gupta
- Grew out of an earlier venture called **Basil Box**
- Positioned as Prayagraj's first authentic Italian fine-dining restaurant
- Claims: handmade raviolis; wood-fired pizza on 00 Napoletana flour; fresh
  mozzarella; sauces from imported Italian peeled tomatoes; exotic vegetables
  and authentic Italian cheeses; in-house herb garden
- Interiors: warm European-style ambiance
- Staff trained to explain ingredient origins and dish backstories
- Stated plans for chef's specials and seasonal menus
