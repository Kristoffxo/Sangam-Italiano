    Read this before you swap these out

    These are drawings, on purpose

    No licensable photography of Sangam Italiano exists yet. The pictures on the Google listing were taken by customers — they belong to those customers, not the restaurant, and publishing them here would be infringement. Generic pizza photos from a stock library would be worse: they'd show food this kitchen never cooked.

    So every plate here is drawn in SVG. It's honest, it weighs nothing, and it stays sharp on any screen.

    To put real photos in

    1. Ask Chef Kushal Gupta for the original files — the restaurant already owns the photography on its own Instagram. Originals, not Instagram downloads, which are re-compressed.
    2. Drop them into an assets/ folder.
    3. Add a photo path to that dish's entry in galleria.js. Each tile already checks for one and will use it instead of the drawing — no other change needed.

    If you'd rather shoot fresh: six frames carry a restaurant site — exterior at dusk, the oven mid-service, the sofa swing, two plated dishes, one wide shot of the room.
